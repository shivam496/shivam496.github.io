❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add clothes to my wardrobe?
Simply tap the + button and take a photo of your clothing item. Our AI will automatically remove the background and categorize it for you.
Is my data stored in the cloud?
All your wardrobe data is stored locally on your device. We prioritize your privacy and don't upload your photos to any external servers.
How does weather-based suggestion work?
Kloset uses your location to get current weather data and suggests outfits appropriate for the temperature and conditions.
Can I export my wardrobe data?
Yes! Go to Settings > Data Management to export your wardrobe data as a backup or transfer to a new device.
How do I delete my data?
You can delete individual items by swiping left, or clear all data from Settings > Data Management > Delete All Data.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 28, 2026

Overview

Kloset ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

Local Data Only: Kloset stores all your wardrobe data locally on your device. We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal photos or wardrobe information to external servers.

The app may collect:

  • Location Data: Used solely for weather-based outfit suggestions. This data is not stored or shared.
  • Camera Access: Required to photograph clothing items. Photos are processed and stored locally on your device only.
  • Anonymous Analytics: Basic app usage statistics (crashes, feature usage) to help us improve the app. No personal information is included.

How We Use Your Information

  • Provide weather-appropriate outfit recommendations
  • Improve app performance and user experience
  • Fix bugs and technical issues

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to third parties. Your wardrobe data remains on your device and under your control.

Data Security

Your data is stored securely on your device using iOS's built-in encryption. We recommend keeping your device passcode enabled for additional security.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access all your data within the app
  • Export your data at any time
  • Delete all your data from the app

Children's Privacy

Kloset is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy in the app and updating the "Last updated" date.
